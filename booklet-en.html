02 · CAPABILITIES

What eff computes

Seven layers of analysis, boiled down to one number you can act on.

01

The fitness score

Age, reliability, efficiency, capacity, maintenance cost, condition, spare-parts availability, and safety combine into one score out of 100 — no machine learning.
02

Does replacing it save money?

Comparing the cost of keeping the machine against replacing it — the real difference only. The most this test can recommend is "plan for replacement", never "retire now".
03

Daily energy-burn ledger

One daily EGP number, split into maintenance's share (wear) and operations' share (misallocated load).
04

Matching the machine to the job

A sound machine on the wrong job is an operations problem to fix by reassignment — never a reason to replace it.
05

Emissions, from the same numbers

Carbon footprint calculated from the same excess-burn numbers in the energy ledger — never a separate estimate.
06

Product carbon footprint, by region

Carbon per tonne of product, and how far from the best achievable in your region — water depth and irrigation distance vary by location.
07

A fee calculated for you

The fee is set by the actual effort your fleet requires — assets, sites, data readiness.

05 · PRICING CRITERIA

How fees are calculated

There is no fixed price list. Every quote is computed from declared factors, so any two clients with the same profile receive the same number.

The formula

days  = setup + (assets × per_asset) + (sites × per_site) + analysis + reporting
days ×= data_readiness × urgency × diversity × language
fee   = days × day_rate − discounts

Effort factors

ComponentDaysWhat it covers
Setup0.50Scoping call and configuring the data sheet for the sector
Per asset0.03Reviewing the machine's data, chasing gaps, interpreting its result
Per site0.25Coordination with and follow-up of the site coordinator
Analysis0.50Running the methodology and reviewing outliers

The value check

A defensible fee lands between 1% and 3% of the fleet's annual energy bill. Outside that band the quote is reviewed. This is a sanity check, not a pricing rule — it flags, it never silently changes the number.
