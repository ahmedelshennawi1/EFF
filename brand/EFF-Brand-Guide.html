A brand built like the product: an instrument, not an agency. Dark-first, one hot accent, numbers as the hero.

01The mark

Three descending bars. It is the skeleton both letterforms share — E and F are stacked horizontal strokes — and it is a falling meter reading: consumption, fitness, burn. One shape, two readings, both true to the product. Draw it with geometry, never with a font.

The wordmark is lowercase, always. "eff" reads as a product; "EFF" reads as a committee. In running Arabic text the name stays in Latin script (منصة eff) — no «إي إف إف». Clearspace around the mark: the height of one bar on all sides. Minimum mark size 20px; below that, use the bars alone.

The collision rule. Ember is orange; "Plan replacement" is red-clay. They must never compete: ember appears only on interactive and brand elements (buttons, links, KPI figures, the mark); verdict colors appear only on data (pills, bars, table cells). An element is never both. If a layout puts them side by side, the verdict wins and ember retreats to a neutral.

03Typography

One family for both scripts: Rubik carries Latin and Arabic in a single design, so bilingual screens stay one voice — no bolted-on Arabic face. Data always in IBM Plex Mono, tabular. (This guide renders in system fallbacks; production embeds Rubik + Plex Mono.)

Numbers are the hero. Western digits (123) in both languages — the unit strings (L/kWh, EGP) make mixed numerals unreadable. Tabular figures wherever numbers stack. Money and scores may take ember; body text never does.

04Voice

An instrument speaks in readings, not adjectives. Every claim carries its number; every number carries its basis.

Say

"GEN-02 burns EGP 961/day above baseline — replacing it saves EGP 1,043,488 a year."
«المولد يحرق 961 جنيهًا يوميًا فوق خط الأساس.»

Never

"Cutting-edge AI-powered asset optimization solutions" — no superlatives, no "solutions", no unexplained percentages, and never a verdict the data cannot carry.

06Never

  • Uppercase the wordmark, transliterate it to Arabic script, or set it in another font.
  • Use ember on verdict data, or verdict colors on buttons and links.
  • Gradients, glassmorphism, purple — this is an instrument, not a landing-page trend.
  • Show a percentage or score without its basis (confidence, baseline, or benchmark).
  • Borrow the Ahmed Elshennawi teal identity — eff is a separate company with its own face. The consulting brand may refer to eff; it never wraps it.
